# Follow both legs of your journey: a walkthrough for a first-time tester

Written 28 September 2026 for someone who has not used Lost Minutes before. It takes about ten
minutes on a phone. Nothing here has been tried by a real passenger yet; that is what this is for.

## What you need

- A phone with a web browser (the site works in the browser, nothing to install).
- A weekday or Saturday between about 07:00 and 22:00, when buses are running.
- The address: **https://lost-minutes.duckdns.org**

You do not need to share your location. Everything below works from a typed starting point.

## The journey to try

**From Hillingdon Road (Stretford) to MediaCityUK.** No single bus goes there; the site should
offer the 263 (or the 255) towards Piccadilly Gardens to Trafford Bar, a short walk across the
interchange (about 3 minutes), and the 53 to MediaCityUK.

1. Open the site. Tap **Plan** (top right of the map).
2. Tap the **From** box, type `hillingdon road`, and choose **Hillingdon Road (nr)**.
3. In **To**, type `mediacityuk` and choose **MediaCityUK (at)**.
4. The site lists journeys, soonest there first. Each one says when its next connection leaves and
   arrives by the timetable ("Next: 255 06:42 from Davyhulme Road East (nr) · at MediaCityUK
   07:03"), which bus to take first, where to get off, where to walk to, and which bus to take next.
   Tap **Choose this journey** on the first one.

## Then, without anyone's help, try to answer these

Write your answers down before reading on.

1. **Which bus do I board first, and from which stop?**
2. **Where do I get off it, and where do I walk to?**
3. **Which bus do I take next, and towards where?**
4. **Are the times on the card scheduled or predicted?** (One word on the card answers this.)
5. **What can I do if I miss the change?**
6. **Is any bus actually being tracked right now, and how do you know?**

Expected answers, for whoever runs the test. At most hours the first journey is the one below; at
some the 250 from the same change, or a journey through the city centre, comes first, and the
answers follow the card: (1) the 263 or the 255 towards Piccadilly Gardens, from Davyhulme Road
East (nr); (2) Trafford Bar (opp), then walk to Trafford Bar (by), about 3 minutes; (3) the 53
towards Salford Shopping Centre, to MediaCityUK (at); (4) scheduled: the list says "by the
timetable, not live", and the card "Times are the operators’ timetables … not adjusted for where
the buses are"; (5) the rows under the steps list the next connections by the timetable, a row
can say an earlier bus makes the same 53, **More time to change** (under Details) adds five
minutes, and **Other journeys** lists the alternatives; (6) under each bus the card says either
"Tracked on this journey · N stops before …", "A 255 is tracked … · the 06:19 by the timetable"
(a bus on another journey, named by its time), "A 53 is tracked … which journey it is on is not
identified", or "No tracked bus … yet · that is not ‘no bus’", and the map draws every tracked bus.

## Things to try next

- **Show whole journey / First bus / Next bus** under the card: the map should frame that part and
  then stay put. New positions arriving must not move the map by themselves.
- Tap **I’m on the first bus**. The card should now lead with getting off at Trafford Bar and the
  walk, and its times become the next 53s from Trafford Bar (by); the page's stop becomes Trafford
  Bar (opp). **Not on it yet** goes back.
- Tap **I’ve changed buses**. The card should lead with getting off at MediaCityUK, with no more
  times to catch.
- Reload the page. The journey, and the stage you were at, should come back.
- Tap **Ride along** on the map (a tracked bus is needed). The card over the map should keep the
  other bus in view ("Next bus: the 53 …") with **Focus on the next bus** or **Show the next bus’s
  stop on the map**.
- Tap **Details** at the foot of the card. This is where the technical account lives: how a bus is
  tied to a timetabled journey, that the walk was checked with a router, that accessibility of
  the change is not known.
- Tap **Share**. The text and link name the journey and the destination, never your location.
- Tap **End** to finish.

## What to report back

- Any question above you could not answer from the card alone.
- Any moment the map moved without you touching it.
- Any wording that read as a promise ("the bus will arrive at …") rather than a timetable.
- Whether the walk at Trafford Bar matched what the card said (minutes and metres).
- Phone model, browser, and whether you used your real location.
